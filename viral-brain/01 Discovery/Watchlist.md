## Selection guidelines
Pick a **diverse** sample: new/small creators, established creators, different posting dates, flop/control examples, and video styles. Avoid collecting only huge accounts or only clips that already went viral, because that makes patterns hard to interpret.

Record *when* you saw a metric. A video at 100,000 views after one hour is very different from the same total after one year.
