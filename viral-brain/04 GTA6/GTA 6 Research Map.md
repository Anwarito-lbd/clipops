# GTA 6 — viral edit research map

Focus on reproducible **editing mechanics** rather than copying other creators' footage or proprietary audio.

## Edit families to study
1. **Cinematic montage:** color, framing, pacing, sound design, jump-cut timing.
2. **Character-first edit:** Lucia / Jason focus, expression-to-action transitions, narrative text.
3. **Vice City atmosphere:** establishing shots, neon/color matching, sound bridges.
4. **Trailer breakdown:** fact-based sequence and commentary, official source vs fan speculation.
5. **Reaction clip:** a streamer reacting to a reveal or game moment; event + face + subtitles.
6. **Comparison edit:** GTA V vs GTA VI, labelled and sourced.
7. **Hype/countdown:** anticipation builds with a clear payoff; avoid asserting unverified release claims.
8. **Comedy / expectation vs reality:** concise setup followed by reversal.

## Classification checklist
- [ ] Is it official GTA VI media or a fan edit?
- [ ] Does it show actual gameplay, a trailer, a concept, AI art, or unverified footage?
- [ ] What is the hook in the first two seconds?
- [ ] What is the core emotional trigger: surprise, comedy, nostalgia, satisfaction, controversy?
- [ ] Where is the beat drop / cut / reveal?
- [ ] What is the reuse-permission status?

## Research tasks
Collect 10 different GTA 6 edits (not only the top-ranked ones). Capture complete observations; then compare their length, opening hooks, clip structures and share rate where numbers are available. Use [[01 Discovery/Research Protocol]].
