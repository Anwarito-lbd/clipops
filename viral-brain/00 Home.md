## Daily loop (20–30 minutes)
1. Browse a selected topic for 10 minutes and copy 3–5 real video links.
2. Record the available numbers and their observation date. Do not guess missing metrics.
3. Watch each video and fill in the first-second hook, edit rhythm, payoff, captions, audio, and loop.
4. Link each clip note to a pattern. Update patterns only when evidence recurs across distinct examples.
5. Test one new rule with **your own or authorized footage**. Log results in [[05 Experiments/Experiment Board]].

## Core questions
- What happens at 0–2 seconds to stop the scroll?
- How many seconds until the viewer understands the setup?
- What is the key reveal, shock, joke, transformation or payoff?
- Where does the music beat land relative to the reveal?
- Does the last frame create a loop or an invitation to comment?
- Could the effect translate to a Twitch clip with permission?

## Limits
TikTok access is not connected in this vault. There is no automatic feed crawler here. All sample patterns are **hypotheses to validate**, not a claim that any specific GTA 6 edit was watched or verified viral.
